About us

Four machines for restricted-access groundwork, sold by people who will give you the measurements

IronVant is a UK online retailer of compact plant for site crews: a 705 mm mini excavator, a 500 kg tracked dumper, a two-wheel petrol wood chipper and a Honda-powered stump grinder. We picked four machines that fit through the gaps small sites leave you, and nothing else.

We are not a manufacturer and we do not run a warehouse. Every machine ships crated on a pallet direct from our supplier's warehouse overseas and reaches the UK mainland in 7–12 working days, delivery included. The price on the page is the price you pay — there is nothing further to pay on delivery.

What we add is the part that usually goes missing: published measurements, a 30-day return, two years on parts and a phone line answered Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm.
